    TUTORIAL

    Welcome to GOLNET (Game Of Life NETwork), a multiplayer version of Conway's Game of Life!

    The Goal of the Game

    The goal is simple: conquer as much territory as possible by placing cells of your color. With each "tick" (time interval), the grid evolves according to specific rules:

    • A living cell with 2 or 3 neighbors survives.
    • A dead cell with exactly 3 neighbors is born (and takes the color of the majority player among its neighbors).
    • In all other cases, a cell dies (from loneliness or overpopulation).

    Your score is the number of cells of your color. The game ends when the timer runs out. Be strategic to make your patterns grow and invade others'!

    CONTROLS

      Mouse

    • Left Click: Place / Remove a preview cell.
    • Right Click (hold): Pan the camera across the grid.
    • Scroll Wheel: Zoom in / Zoom out.

      • Keyboard

    • Z, Q, S, D or W, A, S, D: Move the camera.
    • Esc: Cancel the placement of a selected pattern.

      • Combinations

    • Shift + Left Click (drag): "Paint" or "erase" preview cells continuously.

      • Touch Screen

    • 1 Finger (drag): Pan the camera.
    • 2 Fingers (pinch/stretch): Zoom in / Zoom out.
    • 1 Finger (tap): Place / Remove a preview cell.
